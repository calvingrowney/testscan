# Claim register

Copy the table into a working file and add every factual-sounding phrase before it appears in a concept label, website, creator brief, social post, or ad.

| Exact wording / visual implication | Where used | What a buyer may understand | Claim type | Exact evidence needed | Evidence on file? | Reviewer / status | Temporary safe wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example: "Protein-packed" | Homepage hero | This is high in protein | Nutrition / implied nutrient level | Exact finished-product nutrition analysis and compliant use review | No | Needs qualified review | "Contains [verified] g protein per serving" or remove |

## Status labels

- **Idea only:** not approved for any public-facing use.
- **Evidence gathered:** documentation exists but not professionally reviewed.
- **Professionally reviewed:** specify reviewer, date, scope, and limitations.
- **Approved for specific use:** specify exact label/ad/page and version.
- **Removed:** do not reuse without reopening review.
